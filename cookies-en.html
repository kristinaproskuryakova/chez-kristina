1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small file saved on your device when you visit a website. It lets the website remember certain information, such as your preferences.

2. Cookies used on this website

Necessary cookies: they make the website work properly by remembering your chosen language (French or English) and your cookie choice. They are stored in your browser (local storage) and do not require your consent.

Google Maps: the interactive map at the bottom of the home page is provided by Google Ireland Ltd. When it is shown, Google may set cookies and receive your IP address. The map is loaded only with your consent; without it, a simple link to directions is offered. Learn more: Google's cookie policy.

Analytics cookies: the website does not currently use any. [To be completed if an analytics tool is added: name of the tool and retention period.]

Third-party content without cookies: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and the menu from Google Sheets. These services may receive your IP address, but do not set cookies through this website.

3. Your consent

On your first visit, a window lets you accept, reject or choose non-essential cookies. Rejecting is as easy as accepting, and rejecting does not prevent you from using the website. You can change your choice at any time with the “Manage cookies” link at the bottom of the home page.

4. Retention period

Your choice (consent or refusal) is kept for 6 months, after which you will be asked again. Cookies set by Google follow the periods set by Google, up to 13 months in line with CNIL recommendations.
